TalkBoard AAC — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

Short version: TalkBoard keeps everything on your device. We don't collect, store, sell or share any personal information, and there are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

What the app stores, and where

Your communication boards, buttons, photos you add, settings, recent messages and the optional child profile questionnaire are saved only in the app's storage on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app (or clearing the browser's site data) deletes them.

Photos

If you add a photo to a button, the app shrinks it and keeps it on the device. The app only sees photos you choose. It does not use the camera, microphone or location on its own.

Speech

Speech uses your device's built-in text-to-speech. Some voices, such as voices marked 🌐 in Settings, are provided by your device's operating system or browser and may use the internet; those services follow their own privacy policies (Apple, Google, Microsoft). Voices marked ✈️ are installed on the device.

Backups and sharing

"Copy backup" puts your vocabulary and settings into a text box or on your clipboard so you can save or move them. The app never sends a backup anywhere. Naming a teacher, therapist or other person in the questionnaire does not invite, notify or give them access.

Children's privacy

TalkBoard is designed to be used by children and adults with their families and support teams. Because we don't collect any personal information from anyone, we don't collect personal information from children. The app has no ads, in-app purchases, chat with strangers or links to social media.

Hosting

The web version is delivered by our hosting provider (Netlify), which may keep standard technical server logs (such as IP address and time of the request) to deliver and secure the site. We don't use these logs to identify you.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date.
