No sound?

Turn the device volume up and check it isn't on silent. In Settings → Voice, press ▶ Test. If no voices are listed, add voices on the device: iPhone/iPad: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices. Android: Settings → Accessibility → Text-to-speech → Install voice data. Windows: Settings → Time & language → Speech → Add voices. Chromebook: Settings → Accessibility → Text-to-speech.

Moving to a new device

On the old device: Settings → Copy backup, and save the text (for example, email it to yourself). On the new device: paste it in Settings → Restore.

Stop changes by accident

Settings → turn on "Communication-only mode" and/or "Require code". Hold ⚙️ for 2 seconds to get back to Settings.

Paper backup

Settings → Printable boards.

Privacy

Everything stays on your device. Read the privacy policy.
